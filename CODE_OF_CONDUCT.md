# Community code of conduct / Convivencia

We welcome respectful participation in English and Spanish. Discuss ideas and evidence, not personal characteristics. Give constructive feedback, respect differences, credit contributors, protect private information and obtain permission before sharing others' work.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, deliberate disruption, doxxing and disclosure of private data are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, limit participation or block accounts when needed. Decisions should be proportionate, based on behavior and documented privately when appropriate.

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through a contact channel they make available, or use GitHub's abuse reporting tools. Do not expose victims' personal details in public issues. Security vulnerabilities follow SECURITY.md instead. Maintainers should avoid retaliation and disclose conflicts of interest when handling reports.

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and official community spaces.
